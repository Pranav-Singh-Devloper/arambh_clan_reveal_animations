❓ WHAT are the Rishihood Clans?
Your Campus Identity: The entire Rishihood learner body is divided into 4 distinct Clans. Rajputana, Maratha, Chola & Vijaya
More Than Just a Group: Your Clan is your home base for campus rivalries, sports leagues, cultural fests, and lifelong friendships.
🤔 WHY are you assigned a Clan?
Instant Connections: Skip the awkward icebreakers; you immediately belong to a tight-knit family of peers and seniors.
Faculty Mentorship: You’ll share your Clan with faculty members and staff who will cheer for you both in and out of class!
💡 HOW does this enrich your university life?
Friendly Competition: Earn Clan points through academics, sports, arts, and community initiatives to win the Clan Glory Cup and much more!
Beyond the Classroom: Build leadership skills, collaborate across different majors, and forge bonds that drive incredible classroom synergy.
A Built-in Support System: Seniors in your Clan are your go-to guides for navigating campus life.
