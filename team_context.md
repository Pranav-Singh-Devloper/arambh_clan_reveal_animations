❓ WHAT is happening?
One Community, Four Clans: All Rishihood learners are divided into 4 vibrant Clans to foster competition, collaboration, and community spirit. Vijaya, Rajputana, Chola and Maratha.
Expanding the Circle: All faculty and team members are divided into these Clans to build a unified Rishihood family!
🤔 WHY should I know my Clan?
Integration with Learning: Incorporate Clan dynamics into your classroom activities, group projects, and team exercises.
Shared Ownership: Clan spirit isn't just managed by the Learner Experience Team; it belongs to all of us!
💡 HOW is this helpful for me?
Stronger Learner Connection: Connect with learners outside academics, breaking formal barriers.
Enhanced Classroom Camaraderie: Pre-existing Clan bonds create better engagement, participation, and trust inside the classroom. They have their slogans, rituals and camaraderie!
Cross-Departmental Synergy: Team up with colleagues from different departments under your shared Clan banner for classroom activities. 
🎯 WHAT can you do next?
Find Your Clan: Click the button above and discover where you belong.
Wear Your Colours: Represent your Clan during university events, sports days, and campus drives. Email us @ learnerexperience@rishihood.edu.in to get one!
Bring it to the Classroom: Design fun icebreakers, quizzes, or team challenges around Clan points!
✨ Let’s build a stronger, more connected Rishihood together!
